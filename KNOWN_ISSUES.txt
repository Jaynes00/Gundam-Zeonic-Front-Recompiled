GUNDAM ZEONIC FRONT RECOMPILED 1.0 (1.0.0): KNOWN ISSUES AND OPEN CHECKS
=========================================================================

This build has NOT been checked against the PS2 original by side-by-side
comparison. Much of it was verified against the original game code, and by
automated tests, but complete retail accuracy is not claimed. Your reports
are what will confirm or correct it.


NEW IN 1.0 (please confirm in play)
-----------------------------------
- SCREEN TRANSITIONS IN MISSIONS. ABORT MISSION and RETRY now end the
  mission with the PS2's "tearing" effect (the picture pulled apart in
  strips) instead of a plain fade to black, and a retried mission tears in
  from the right. The mission start, the end of the results screen and the
  story scene after a mission use the same effects. These follow the
  original game code; please tell us if they look different from your PS2.
- WIDESCREEN 16:9: the black tiles of the mission-complete fade now cover
  the whole wide screen while they shrink away (before, the side areas
  showed the bright scene for a few seconds). The tearing transitions cover
  the 4:3 picture; the side areas fade to black with it.
- The lower HUD (radar, command panel, map) stays together in the middle at
  16:9, as in the 4:3 layout.
- LAUNCHER: tidier window (one large PLAY button, the tools in one row,
  green / red status, progress bar only while the disc is checked).


NEW IN RC5.1 (please confirm in play)
-------------------------------------
- TRAINING PROGRAMS. Answering YES to the training offer before Round 1
  Missions 1-3 now starts the two training programs of that mission, as on
  the PS2, with their instructions, the highlighted targets and the final
  question. Afterwards your campaign continues exactly where it was (the
  campaign data is restored unchanged). The training programs can also be
  chosen from the SIMULATOR list once they are open. NO goes straight to
  the briefing, as before.
  Please tell us if a training lesson cannot be finished: the automated
  tests finished programs 1, 2 and 5, but the combat and squad-order
  exercises of programs 3, 4 and 6 were not finished by the test player.
- WIDESCREEN 16:9: target brackets now also appear on enemies in the extra
  side areas, the attack warnings sit at the screen edges, and the beam
  saber trail and heat haze are drawn there too. HUD pieces that the PS2
  keeps off screen no longer show in the side areas (and a dark wedge that
  appeared in the sonar display in 16:9 is gone). Lock-on still works only
  in the original area, so gameplay does not change.
- MOVIES: the SPECIAL movie's sound no longer drops out and then lags
  behind the picture in places. Movies are always shown 4:3, never
  stretched.
- Mission 3: the mission clock appears on the screen at the moment the
  radio message calls for it, as on the PS2.
- A debris effect used by some Round 2 enemies (Missions 1 and 4) is now
  drawn.
- Mission 9 (both rounds): unchanged and confirmed again. It ends the
  moment the last enemy falls, the nine gun emplacements included (several
  stand at the map edges). If one is left, the mission ends when the tanks
  arrive. In our automated play-through four edge emplacements were left
  and the tanks ended the mission - most likely what was reported.
- Still open: see the sections below. Nothing in them was removed.


NEW IN RC5 FINAL (please confirm in play)
-----------------------------------------
- MOVIES. The original movies now play: the BANDAI logo and the opening at
  start-up, the attract demos when the title screen is left alone, the
  ending and the staff roll after the campaign, and the MOVIE menu (SPECIAL,
  OPENING; ENDING and STAFF once the game is cleared). As on the PS2, START
  skips a movie, except the ending, and the staff roll the first time.
  The movies need the MPEG-2 video decoder that comes with Windows. On
  Windows "N" editions without the Media Feature Pack they are skipped.
  A disc folder prepared by an earlier test build is copied again once,
  because the six movie files (about 1.4 GB) are added.
- SPECIAL is in the MOVIE menu from the start. That is how the US PS2 game
  does it (it is not an unlockable).
- WIDESCREEN 16:9 (F10 -> ASPECT RATIO). The 3D view gets wider at the
  sides with the same proportions. The HUD and the menus keep their original
  place in the middle, and menus and movies are pillarboxed. Gameplay is
  unchanged. (RC5.1: target brackets now also appear for units in the side
  areas, and the beam saber trail and heat haze are drawn there; see above.)
  4:3 stays the original look and is the default.
- RESOLUTION (internal 1x-4x), DISPLAY MODE (window / borderless fullscreen,
  also Alt+Enter) and WINDOW SIZE presets (1024x768 to 3840x2160) in the
  F10 menu. All are remembered.
- (Fixed in RC5.1: answering YES to the training offer now starts the
  training programs.)


NEW IN RC5 (please confirm in play)
-----------------------------------
- VOLUME SLIDER in the launcher (0-100 %). It is remembered, it is applied
  before the game makes any sound, and moving it while the game runs
  changes the volume within a moment. It only changes the game's own
  sound: the Windows volume and other programs are not touched.
- ENEMY EQUIPMENT now works. Some Round 2 enemies (and some simulator
  programs) carry devices the port never used before:
    Round 2 Mission 4: the GMs throw a device that disables your suit -
      the screen dims, the radar fills with static and "MACHINE TROUBLE"
      blinks. This is the original behaviour; Round 1 Mission 4 has no
      such enemies.
    Round 2 Missions 5 and 8: stun devices.
    Round 2 Mission 9: knock-down devices.
  Please tell us whether it looks and times like the PS2.
- Round 2 Mission 8: the opening now hands you control at the same time
  as in Round 1 (it used to take about 5 seconds longer).
- SUPPORT menu in battle: with 8 to 10 support items the last rows were
  drawn empty inside the menu frame. All ten rows are drawn now.
- Mission 9 (both rounds) ends with "Enemy destroyed!" the moment the last
  enemy falls, even before the tanks arrive - exactly as the original.
  This includes the nine fixed gun emplacements spread over the map
  (corners of the map too): if the mission did not end for you, one of
  them (or another enemy) was probably still standing. If you see it
  differently on the PS2, please send your save.
- Lt. Sandra's NEW marker (Mission 10): in the original rules a newly
  joined pilot is marked NEW on the pilot only; her suit is her standard
  suit, so it gets no NEW. Suits that are new for a pilot do show NEW in

- "Damage marks" on suits destroyed in melee: the port shows the
  original's hit marks for melee kills. If you mean a different mark,
  a PS2 screenshot would help us find it.


NEW IN RC4: WHY SQUADMATES SOMETIMES DO NOT SHOOT (please read)
---------------------------------------------------------------
- The squad panel's ADVANCE setting is two original switches, set by your
  route points or the attitude keys:
    the arrows   ">>>" = run, ">" = walk
    ATTACK / AVOID
  ATTACK: the squad closes in on enemies it knows about and fires when in
  range; if an enemy gets very close it will use melee.
  AVOID: the squad keeps to its route and only fires at enemies it sees
  itself inside its weapon's range; it does not move toward them.
  In tests, a Raketen squad set to AVOID with the enemy 18 cells away never
  fired; set to ATTACK it closed in and fired. If your squadmates "refuse to
  fire", check whether the panel says AVOID.
- The original game has no third "level" for these: only ">>>" / ">" and
  ATTACK / AVOID. The level 1-3 squad commands (radar / thermal / sonar
  boosts and jammers) change sensors, not attacking.
- Every pilot's squadmates were tested: all fire. Better pilots react
  faster (first shot after about 1 to 3 seconds; weaker ones up to 6).


NEW IN RC3 (please confirm in play)
-----------------------------------
- Squad command panel (bottom right): each team now shows its real state
  (WAIT when it is waiting or holding, BATTLE / battle codes when engaged,
  MOVE only while it moves). A team that was not deployed shows no label.
  Until RC3 every panel said MOVE for the whole mission.
- Squadmate combat was audited for every original suit / weapon pairing
  (92 pairs): every pairing detects, fires, uses ammunition, changes
  magazines and reloads like the original. No defect was found in that
  chain. The original rules that can make a squadmate hold fire are:
  it only fires at enemies it sees itself (its own field of view, not your
  sensor), only inside its weapon's reach (machine guns 15 cells), and
  bazookas pause for long reloads (a close enemy then gets a melee attack).
  If a squadmate still refuses to fire, please note the mission, the suit,
  the weapon, how far the enemy was and whether it was in front of it.
- NEW markers in TEAM SETUP: they appear where the original puts them (for
  example CPO Manning and Lt. Renchef at Mission 7) and blink, but they are
  faint. Please tell us whether they look brighter on the PS2.


FIXED SINCE RC1 (please confirm in play)
----------------------------------------
- Sound breaking up, sounds cut off, tank / helicopter sounds that never stop,
  and the pause-menu chime playing only every other time. Cause: with a draw
  distance above "Original", far-away vehicles left a looping sound behind
  every frame until all 16 sound channels were used up.
- Mobile suit explosions now have their explosion sound (and the original
  screen flash and camera shake). Burning wrecks crackle.
- Squad radio voices: your squadmates' radio calls and the battle code
  announcements ("Code Alpha" and so on) are now heard.
- The sensor modes have their own background sound, and the sensor contact
  alarm sounds when an enemy is strongly detected.
- Mission 8 crash (heat haze effect) fixed.
- SELECT (gamepad View / Back, keyboard Backspace) opens the full battlefield
  map.
- In the semi-auto squad view, the D-pad overhead view can now be turned and
  zoomed out (right stick), as on the PS2.
- Enemy information: the lines to the target are white again, the corner
  marks are coloured by alert level.
- Unidentified mobile suits show "- no data -" again after a simulator
  program has been played (the US disc uses "- no data -", not "???").
- Simulator programs are announced with their own number (was "program 00").
- The results screen darkens the scene behind it again; the separator line,
  the pause-menu frame and dimming, and the map window frame are drawn again.


AWAITING HUMAN COMPARISON WITH THE PS2 (please tell us how they look)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- THERMAL view now darkens the battlefield with the original's blue screen
  tint (the HUD is not tinted), recovered from the game code after your PS2
  screenshots. Please compare it with the PS2.
- Night Scope: on night missions it raises the visual range and, while any
  sensor (RADAR / THERMAL / SONAR) is switched on, the mobile suits are drawn
  lit in cyan and without fog, as in the PS2 screenshots. There is no green
  effect in the original code.
- Camera cuts show one or two black frames, as the original does.
- Raketen Bazooka: the original weapon data is 6 rockets per magazine and 2
  magazines, then a long reload (about 15 seconds between magazines and about
  60 seconds after the second). Squadmates with it fire, then wait out these
  reloads. Squadmates also only fire at enemies they can see themselves. If
  one stays idle for longer with an enemy in front of it, please note the
  mission, suit and how long.
- Mission 6 ending: the Gundam / Guntank / Guncannon on the cliff are lit by
  the mission's dusk light. If they look different on the PS2, a screenshot of
  that shot would help.
- The green frame around the target's HP bar.
- Squadmate behaviour (formation, holding position, not attacking): automated
  checks of every mission in both rounds found squadmates firing whenever a
  detected target is within their weapon's reach. If a squadmate still does
  nothing while an enemy is close and visible, please note the mission, the
  squad and its orders.
- Screen transitions inside missions are still plain fades.
- The music fade-out length when a menu closes.

KNOWN DIFFERENCES / LIMITS
--------------------------
- Round 2, Mission 8: allied squads can be slow to start moving at the
  beginning of the mission. This is known and deferred; please report it only
  if the mission cannot be finished.
- Esc closes the game immediately, without asking. Save first.
- While an XInput gamepad is connected, the game reads only the gamepad: the
  keyboard's game keys do nothing until the gamepad is disconnected (the PC
  keys F6 to F10 and Esc still work).


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "This disc is not supported": only the US release (SLUS-20233) works.
  Japanese discs and modified or patched images are refused.
- "does not match the retail release": the image is damaged. Make a fresh
  copy of your disc.
- The game does not start: make sure the whole folder was extracted (the .dll
  files must be next to zf_view.exe), then create a bug report.
